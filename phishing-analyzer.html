PhishTrace

Client-side heuristic analysis of email headers & body — nothing leaves your browser

Raw email source

All parsing happens locally in JavaScript. No network request is made with your pasted content.

Findings

Paste an email on the left and click Analyze email.

PhishTrace checks sender/reply-to mismatches, link-vs-display spoofing, IP-literal and punycode URLs, URL shorteners, urgency and credential-harvesting language, spoofed authentication headers, and risky attachment types — then gives a weighted risk score with a breakdown of every flag.
Heuristic tool for education and triage support — not a substitute for your organization's mail security gateway. A low score does not guarantee an email is safe; a high score does not guarantee it is malicious. When in doubt, report to your security team.
